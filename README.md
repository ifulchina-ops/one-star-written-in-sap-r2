A one-line 1-star review from a houseplant about its human owner (161 characters). The plant's grievance is the whole joke: repotted into a coffee mug with no drainage, watered with yesterday's coffee, and its brown leaves dismissed as "character".

> One star. He repotted me into a coffee mug with no drainage, waters me with yesterday's coffee like it's a loyalty scheme, and calls my brown leaves 'character'.

`main.ts` validates the line against the gate (one line, 1–279 characters) and writes it to `/output/entry.txt`, reading it back to prove the written text is byte-for-byte the review.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```

Writes the review to `/output/entry.txt` (override with `ENTRY_OUTPUT`).
